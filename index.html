Going from 27% to 20% means losing about 5 kg of fat while gaining about 2 kg of muscle. At 177 cm and 64 kg you are not carrying excess weight, you are carrying too little muscle. Dieting straight down to 60 kg lands you near 24% and softer than you are now.

Stop targeting 60 kg. Target the waist tape and the photos. Built properly, you may sit at 62 kg and look far leaner than 60 kg would have.

The Eufy body-fat reading is bioimpedance, accurate to roughly ±5% and it moves with hydration. Trend only, never a single number. Realistic timeline for 5 kg of fat at a muscle-sparing rate is 16 to 20 weeks.

Legs on Saturday and Hyrox on Sunday means two hard lower-body days back to back. That is the trade for the weekend you asked for, and it works — but only if Sunday runs at genuinely easy effort. The Sunday list is already rebalanced toward the SkiErg and rower and away from lunges and wall balls, because your quads will not be fresh. If your knees or lower back complain by Week 3, move legs to Friday.

On Monday, Wednesday and Saturday the 30-minute daily block is mobility only, in the evening, nothing loaded. Twelve hours a day at a desk is the single biggest thing working against you. The daily block exists to fight it.

Deload in Week 6 — halve every set count, keep the loads. Test at the end of Weeks 4, 8 and 12.

Desk protocol — costs nothing

Once an hour: stand, 30 seconds of couch stretch each side, 10 shoulder rolls, 5 scapular squeezes. Set a timer. This matters more than any single stretching session you will do.

Maintenance is roughly 2,100 kcal. The deficit is deliberately small — large enough to lose fat, small enough to still build muscle. Expect 0.25 to 0.4 kg per week. Faster than that means losing muscle, which is the opposite of the goal.

Protein anchors: 100 g chicken breast ≈ 31 g · 3 eggs ≈ 18 g · 170 g Greek yoghurt ≈ 17 g · 1 scoop whey ≈ 25 g · 100 g salmon ≈ 25 g · 150 g cottage cheese ≈ 17 g · 100 g cooked lentils ≈ 9 g. Hitting 130 g means protein at every meal plus a shake, not one large protein dinner.

Carbs in the meal before strength work. Water 2.5 to 3 L, more on swim and Hyrox days. Alcohol is the easiest lever — it blunts protein synthesis and adds calories with no fullness.

Hyrox — Sunday

High fatigue, you are a beginner, and it now falls the day after legs. In Phase 1 go at conversational effort and scale everything. This is conditioning, not a second strength session.

  • 800 m run, easy
  • 500 m SkiErg
  • 400 m run
  • 10 wall balls, 4 kg — depth is the point
  • 400 m run
  • 20 burpee step-outs, no jump
  • 400 m run
  • 40 m farmer carry
  • 400 m run
  • 500 m row
  • 10 min cool down, couch stretch both sides

Lunges are deliberately gone and wall balls are cut from 15 to 10, because Saturday already loaded your quads and glutes. Add them back from Week 8 only if Sunday feels easy.

If Sunday leaves you wrecked into Monday, cut it to every other week. Recovery is the limiting factor on a 12-hour desk day, not effort.

Thai massage, TCM and herbs

Keep the weekly Thai massage. Soft-tissue work is genuinely useful for someone sitting twelve hours a day, and Sunday is the right slot.

On herbs, honestly: acupuncture and massage are low-risk and many people find them helpful for tension and sleep. Herbal formulas are a different category. Buy only from a licensed practitioner or an established supplier — adulteration and heavy-metal contamination in unregulated herbal products is a documented problem. Some formulas affect liver enzymes or interact with medications, so tell your practitioner everything you take.

Do not expect herbs to move body composition. Nothing in that category will. Judge them on how you sleep and feel.

The four rules that matter most
  • Protein at 130 g, every day. Everything else in nutrition is negotiable. This is not.
  • Progress the log, not the feeling. Add a rep, a second, or 2.5 kg, and write it down. A session that felt hard but matched last week is not progress.
  • Recovery is the bottleneck, not effort. The failure mode here is doing too much. If sleep drops or sessions get worse two weeks running, cut volume by a third.
  • Stop weighing the goal in kilograms. Waist, photos and the pull-up bar tell the truth.
